# Hyperloop
 Hyperloop is a game where you are a theif that has stolen a car and a AI has taken over. To escape the car, you must beat a variety of puzzles and after completion of each puzzle, certain dialougue text will be displayed on the screen. Hope you enjoy the came!

Walkthrough and Puzzle Solutions- 

Hyperloop opens with your character waking up in the vehicle. The AI will speak to you and then you will be given free range to explore the vehicle. Looking around reveals a mysterious device in the back seat, the mechanism behind the time loop. Additionally, the ceiling contains a key which can be used to solve a later puzzle. 

To start the first puzzle, pick up the rubix cube by overlapping the crosshair and pressing the E key. Follow the on-screen prompt to solve the cube like a traditional cube. 

Solving the rubix cube will unlock the second puzzle. 

The player can move the camera and further explore the car while the second puzzle loads. Simply use WSAD and move the mouse.

The second puzzle is a riddle. Clicking the screen and selecting the text box will allow the player to solve the riddle. The riddle is "What is seen in the middle of March and April that can’t be seen at the beginning or end of either month?". The answer to the riddle is "R". This can be entered in upper or lower case. Press enter to submit your answer. 

Solving the second puzzle will unlock the third puzzle. 

The third puzzle is a cipher. There is a provided key scrolling on the top of the car ceiling. The solution to the cipher is "I am the ultimate ruler in the world.". Ensure that the period is present and there are no additional spaces. Completing this puzzle triggers the final bit of AI dialogue and then will prompt the player with two different endings. Players are encouraged to pick either ending. 

A final cutscene will play and the game will return to the title screen.
